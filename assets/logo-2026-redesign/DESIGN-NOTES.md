# HIRO 2026 Logo Redesign

- This redesign is independent from the earlier `assets/logo-2026/` SVG set.
- The emblem combines ImageGen2 concept A's open composition, concept B's solid clips,
  and concept C's long rear-hair silhouette.
- The nose is the emblem's only curved path.
- `HIRO` is the primary wordmark; `2026` is a separate two-row conference-year block.
- All visible lettering is custom vector path geometry with no font dependency.
